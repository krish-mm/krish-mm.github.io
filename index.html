About

Building self-service research computing at university scale

DevOps engineer with 8+ years automating research computing infrastructure at university scale. I have built self-service portals, CI/CD pipelines, and infrastructure-as-code that cut user onboarding from ~48 hours to under 5 minutes and delivered a zero-downtime filesystem migration for a campus-wide HPC cluster.

  • Built CI/CD pipelines, blue-green deployments, and dev cluster builds for the team.
  • Kept users online during the cluster migration by rerouting traffic through sshpiper and HTTP proxy nodes, so users scheduled to be migrated saw no downtime.
  • Held each migrated user's delta time to no more than 30% of the average.
